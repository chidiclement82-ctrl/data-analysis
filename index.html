Our Story

A small room built around one big fire

Ember & Vine started with a simple idea: cook the best produce we can find over live oak and cherry wood, and pour wines made by people, not factories.

Chef Maya Okafor and sommelier Luca Ferri opened the doors in 2019. The menu still changes with whatever the farmers bring through the back door each week.
