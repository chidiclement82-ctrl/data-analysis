Our Story

Home-style Nigerian food, cooked fresh

Food Is Ready started with a simple idea: cook Nigerian food the way it tastes at home, with fresh ingredients from the market every morning and no shortcuts.

From party jollof to egusi, afang and banga, every pot is made fresh daily. Come and eat with us, or order on WhatsApp and we'll have it ready.
