Good to know

  • Private: your text never leaves your device. The voices come from your phone or computer.
  • More voices: each device has its own. Edge and Chrome have the most natural ones; on phones, add voices in your system's text-to-speech settings.
  • Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘ + Enter plays or pauses, Esc stops.
  • While reading: tap any sentence to jump to it.
